    A day at TLC

    We believe children learn through play. Most of the day happens in discovery centers, with blocks, dramatic play, trains, cars, music and art, and children choose where to play.

    A typical day. Each room keeps its own rhythm, and times vary.

    1. 7:00am

      Doors open

      Children arrive and settle in with free play.

    2. After 8:00am

      Breakfast

      Cereal, muffins, waffles, pancakes or bagels.

    3. Morning

      Circle time

      Talking about the day: letters, colors, shapes and the monthly theme.

    4. Morning

      Discovery centers and snack

      Blocks, trains, dramatic play and sensory play, with fresh fruit or yogurt.

    5. Midday

      Lunch and rest time

      Chicken, pasta, meatballs, pizza or sandwiches, then a quiet rest.

    6. Afternoon

      Specials and active play

      Art, music, sports and gross-motor games, then an afternoon snack.

    7. By 6:00pm

      Pickup

      Families pick up, and the day's art projects go home.

    Specials that visit the classroom

    Guest teachers bring something new every week, all inside our centers.

    • Art

      Ms. Donna brings new materials for keepsake projects that go home.

    • Music

      Ms. Joanne and her accordion, with songs that change each season.

    • Gymnastics

      Strength, flexibility, good listening, and being upside down.

    • Sports

      Soccer and t-ball basics with Coach Ian of Power Surge Sports.

    • Zumba

      Dance moves that make fitness a fun part of every week.

    • Seasonal days

      A fall farm trip, a visit from Santa and the summer carnival.

    Questions parents ask

    What ages do you care for?

    Children from 6 weeks to 5 years, in Infants, Twaddlers, Toddlers, Nursery and Pre-K rooms, plus UPK. In summer, Brookside also welcomes school-age children 5 to 12.

    Are meals included?

    Yes, tuition includes breakfast, lunch and two snacks. Infants and Twaddlers bring age-appropriate food from home. UPK families can send food or join the $100 monthly meal plan.

    Is TLC nut free?

    Yes. Both centers are nut-free facilities, so please keep that in mind for any food you send from home.

    What happens on snow days?

    We follow the Bellmore-Merrick school district for snow closings and delays, and send every decision to parents by text and email.

    Can staff give my child medication?

    Yes. Our director is MAT trained to give medication. Complete the medication consent and allergy forms, and email the director about any services your child receives.

    How do I enroll?

    Book a tour first. After your visit, the director will share the registration, medical and services agreement forms for each child.
